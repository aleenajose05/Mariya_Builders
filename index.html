Who we are

Builders you can trust with your family’s home

Mariya Builders is a construction company based on Market Road, Rajakkad, in the hills of Idukki. We plan, estimate and build homes and buildings that suit the land, the climate and the people who live in them.

  • Complete service — planning, estimation, construction & finishing
  • Experience with hillside and sloping plots across Idukki
  • Honest estimates and quality materials
