FAQ

Questions families often ask

Can’t find your answer? Call us — we’re happy to help.

Contact Now
Can I get only a plan or an estimate, without construction?

Yes. Plan & design and estimation are offered as separate services, so you can take the drawings or the itemised estimate and decide on construction later.

What is included in full material work?

We take care of the whole project — materials, labour, supervision and finishing — from the foundation to handing over the keys, with one point of contact throughout.

Do you take up only sub work or labour contracts?

Yes. If you already have materials or another contractor, our teams can take up individual works such as masonry, plastering, concrete, tiling, stone cladding, painting or compound walls.

Do you build on hillside or sloping plots?

Yes. Much of Idukki is hilly, and we plan foundations, retaining walls, drainage and access to suit sloping land.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based on Market Road, Rajakkad, and take up projects in and around Rajakkad and across Idukki district.

How do I get started?

Just call us on +91 70343 53114 or send a WhatsApp message. We’ll talk about your plot, needs and budget and arrange a site visit.
